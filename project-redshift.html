About

The Story

Reading as

Project Redshift is a hard rock trio from Louth, Lincolnshire, UK. Formed October 2016 by Pete Thickett (bass, lead vocals), Josh Tunnicliffe (guitar, backing vocals), and James Turner (drums). Four singles released 2017–2019: Sanity Catches, Shadow Weather, Serenity, and Crossfire. Josh Tunnicliffe and James Turner departed in 2019; Pete Thickett continues as sole active member driving the 2026 reactivation.

Influences cited: Royal Blood, Queens of the Stone Age, Foo Fighters. Press coverage includes BBC Introducing (Lincolnshire), Maximum Volume Music (Band of the Day, January 2019), Distorted Sound Magazine, ALTCORNER, A&R Factory, and Discovered Magazine. Crossfire received national airplay on Planet Rock Radio. Video production by Loki Films. Press campaign managed by Rage PR.

The band have shared lineups beneath The Hoosiers and Fickle Friends, and played festival stages including JoeFest (BBC Introducing Stage) and Equinox Festival. New single Playing the Game is scheduled for 2026 — their first new music in seven years.

Three mates from Lincolnshire who started a band in 2016, put four singles out, played pubs, a couple of festivals, and opened for some well-known acts — and then life got in the way. Josh and James departed in 2019, leaving Pete to carry the project forward. Seven years later there's new music coming and a story worth telling.

If you've ever liked Royal Blood, QOTSA, or the loud-and-melodic end of British rock, this is for you. Short, tight catalogue: four singles, three videos, one very long pause, and a fifth single landing in 2026.

Playing the Game is the comeback. Stick around.
