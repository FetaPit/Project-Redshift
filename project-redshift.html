Press Bio

Project Redshift is a hard rock trio from Louth, Lincolnshire, UK. Formed October 2016. Four singles released between October 2017 and January 2019: Sanity Catches, Shadow Weather, Serenity, and Crossfire.

Cited influences include Royal Blood, Queens of the Stone Age and Foo Fighters. The band have been praised by BBC Introducing, Maximum Volume Music (Band of the Day, January 2019) and Distorted Sound Magazine. Crossfire received airplay on Planet Rock Radio and was featured by ALTCORNER. The band have shared lineups beneath The Hoosiers and Fickle Friends.

New single Playing the Game is scheduled for 2026 — the band's first new music in seven years.

For Fans

Three mates from Lincolnshire who started a band in 2016, put four singles out, played pubs and a couple of festivals, and then life got in the way. Seven years later there's new music coming — and a story worth telling.

If you've ever liked Royal Blood, QOTSA or the loud-and-melodic end of British rock, this is for you. The catalogue is short and tight: four singles, three music videos, one long pause, and a fifth single about to land.

Playing the Game is the comeback. Stick around.
